Our purpose

More than a game.
A campus community.

MUGPI Sports Management aims to make sports information easier for students to find and help them discover ways to participate in college activities.

  • Central place for sports categories and activities
  • Tournament information and official notices
  • Student registration interest form
  • Gallery for campus sports memories
  • Promoting teamwork, inclusion and fair play
